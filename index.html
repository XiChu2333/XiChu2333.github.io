About

I work at the intersection of wireless propagation and AI agent systems: making physics-based simulations of the radio environment trustworthy enough to stand in for expensive measurement campaigns. My doctoral research develops AI agent-driven calibration of differentiable ray tracing and self-healing computational electromagnetics (CEM) simulations, supporting low-altitude UAV air-to-ground (A2G) communications. At UiPath (NYSE: PATH), I am a core engineer of the observability platform for Agent Builder, operating agent execution-trace telemetry at up to 40 million requests per day.

My earlier measurement work on low-altitude UAV A2G channels — conducted with Universidad Politécnica de Madrid and Tongji University — has accumulated 200+ citations and continues to be used by UAV wireless research groups in the U.S. and worldwide.

Research Interests

  • Air-to-ground wireless channel measurement and modeling for low-altitude UAV systems
  • AI agent and LLM-driven methods for wireless and electromagnetic engineering
  • Differentiable ray tracing and computational electromagnetics
  • Reliability and observability of production AI agent systems
