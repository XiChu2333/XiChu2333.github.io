Research

I work at the intersection of wireless propagation and AI agent systems: making physics-based simulations of the radio environment trustworthy enough to stand in for expensive measurement campaigns. My doctoral research develops AI agent-driven calibration of differentiable ray tracing and self-healing computational electromagnetics (CEM) simulations, supporting low-altitude UAV air-to-ground (A2G) communications. My earlier measurement work has accumulated 200+ citations and continues to be used by UAV wireless research groups worldwide.
